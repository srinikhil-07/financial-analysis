To the addressee set forth above:
 
We have acted as special counsel to Apple Inc., a California corporation (the “Company”), in connection with the Company’s issuance of $500,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its Floating Rate Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Floating Rate Notes”), $750,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its Floating Rate Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Floating Rate Notes”), $1,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 4.650% Senior Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Senior Notes”), $1,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 4.750% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Senior Notes”), $1,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 4.900% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Senior Notes”), $1,250,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 5.100% Senior Notes due 2033 (the “2033 Senior Notes”) and $2,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount of its 5.350% Senior Notes due 2036 (the “2036 Senior Notes” and, together with the 2028 Floating Rate Notes, the 2029 Floating Rate Notes, the 2028 Senior Notes, the 2029 Senior Notes, the 2031 Senior Notes and the 2033 Senior Notes, the “Notes”), under an Indenture, dated as of November 1, 2024, between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as Trustee, and an officers’ certificate, dated November 9, 2026, setting forth the terms of the Notes (together, the “Indenture”), and pursuant to a registration statement on Form S-3 under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Act”), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “Commission”) and dated November 1, 2024 (Registration No. 333-282937) (as so filed and as amended, the “Registration Statement”), and an underwriting agreement, dated November 5, 2026, between the underwriters named therein and the Company.

With your consent, we have relied upon certificates and other assurances of officers of the Company and others as to factual matters without having independently verified such factual matters.
 
We are opining as to the internal laws of the State of New York and the general corporation law of the State of California, and we express no opinion with respect to the applicability to the opinion expressed herein, or the effect thereon, of the laws of any other jurisdiction or, in the case of California, any other laws, or as to any matters of municipal law or the laws of any local agencies within any state.
 
Subject to the foregoing and the other matters set forth herein, it is our opinion that, as of the date hereof, the Notes have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action of the Company and are the legally valid and binding obligations of the Company, enforceable against the Company in accordance with their terms.
